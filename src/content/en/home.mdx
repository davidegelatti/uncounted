<section id="what-it-is-not" class="home-blocco">
  <div class="testa-blocco"><p class="etichetta">What it is not</p><div><h2>What it doesn’t do, up front</h2></div></div>
  <ul class="schede-non">
    <li><span class="ic-grande ic ic-scale" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>It isn’t a measurement</h3><p>It’s an estimate that depends on stated assumptions: change them and it changes. That’s why every number comes with its range.</p></li>
    <li><span class="ic-grande ic ic-cookie" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>It covers consent only</h3><p>Traffic GA4 misses because of ad blockers or tracking errors isn’t included.</p></li>
    <li><span class="ic-grande ic ic-layers" aria-hidden="true"></span><h3>It isn’t needed if GA4 is already estimating</h3><p>With Consent Mode Advanced and Google’s modeling active, the estimator notices and stops, so nothing is counted twice.</p></li>
  </ul>
  <p class="dopo-schede">It also assumes <strong>opt-in</strong> consent, where tracking starts only after the visitor accepts, as in the EU and the UK. Under opt-out rules, common in the United States, the missing share is usually much smaller: <a href="/en/guide/#faq">more in the Guide</a>.<br /><a href="/en/method/">How the method works</a> · <a href="/en/validation/">How it was tested</a></p>
